## Scope

Applies to medicinal products where the active substance consists of or contains cells or tissues of human origin that have been subject to substantial manipulation or are not intended to be used for the same essential function(s) in the recipient as in the donor.

Covers:
- Non-genetically modified cell therapies (dendritic cells, mesenchymal stem cells, T-cells without genetic modification)
- Tissue-engineered products containing cells
- Ex vivo expanded cells for therapeutic use

Does **not** cover: products regulated solely under tissue/cell transplantation regulations, blood transfusion, solid organs.
