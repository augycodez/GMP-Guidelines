## Scope

The primary EU legal framework for Advanced Therapy Medicinal Products. Chapter IV specifically governs:

- GMP requirements specific to ATMPs (gene therapy, somatic cell therapy, tissue-engineered products, and combined ATMPs)
- Traceability and pharmacovigilance obligations
- Hospital exemption provisions
- Requirements for small/medium-sized enterprises
