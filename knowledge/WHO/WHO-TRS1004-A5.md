## Scope

Revised WHO GMP guidelines specifically for biological medicinal products, covering:

- Cell-culture-derived biologics
- Recombinant proteins
- Biological vaccines and their precursors
- Blood-derived products
- Applicable principles for ATMP manufacturing

Intended for manufacturers in WHO Member States and for WHO Prequalification assessments.
